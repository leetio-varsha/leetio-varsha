## Projects here

AI and agents

- [mastra-hotel-concierge](https://github.com/leetio-varsha/mastra-hotel-concierge) — hotel booking agent: typed tools, memory, a workflow that suspends for human confirmation, deterministic evals and tracing.
- [virtual-tryon-studio](https://github.com/leetio-varsha/virtual-tryon-studio) — Next.js virtual try-on for a clothing store: IDM-VTON, FASHN, Grok, Gemini and GPT Image pipelines with an inpaint editor.

Local speech on macOS
- [Sufler](https://github.com/leetio-varsha/Sufler) — real-time call assistant: whisper.cpp transcribes both sides on-device, a hotkey streams a Claude answer back.
- [echo](https://github.com/leetio-varsha/echo) — self-hosted replacement for Notion AI Meeting Notes: menu-bar app records calls, whisper.cpp transcribes, Claude summarizes into Notion.
- [audiobook-tts](https://github.com/leetio-varsha/audiobook-tts) — ebook to M4B audiobook with local TTS on Apple Silicon: Silero, F5-TTS, Chatterbox, Qwen3-TTS via MLX.
